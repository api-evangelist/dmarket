The Counter-Strike 2 esports Major in Cologne, Germany will happen from June 2 to June 21. Valve has released items specifically for this event: tournament and player stickers. The Cologne 2026 Major stickers have broken the tradition of capsules with random drops, as you can buy specific items in the game store.
