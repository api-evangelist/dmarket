Your profile name on Steam is visible to teammates and opponents in various multiplayer games, including Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2, Team Fortress 2, Rust, and others. It’s the nickname people see during chat communications. In a way, this name identifies your gaming personality and lets others know “oh, it’s that guy I played with earlier.”
